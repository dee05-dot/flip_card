The Central Library

The Central Library at NSUT is a massive academic repository housing over 37,000 volumes of books, 60 multi-domain magazines, and 20 national newspapers. Spanning multiple specialized zones, it features dedicated study halls, a resource-rich Book Bank for semester-long lending, and a modern digital media wing. It provides a quiet, air-conditioned sanctuary for engineering scholars to study and research.
