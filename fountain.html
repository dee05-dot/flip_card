The Campus Fountain

Situated directly in front of the grand red-brick Administrative Block, the landmark fountain is the scenic centerpiece of NSUT’s entrance courtyard. It is framed by beautifully manicured green lawns and vibrant seasonal horticulture, offering a picturesque backdrop for campus photography. Serving as a popular meeting point, the soothing flow of the water enhances the serene, sanctuary-like environment of the university.
