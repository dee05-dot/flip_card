NSUT Aministrative Block

About the Campus

The Administrative Block serves as the iconic focal point of Netaji Subhas University of Technology (NSUT), Delhi. It features distinctive red-brick architecture adorned with a large, intricate mural artwork on its facade.

Fronted by a beautifully landscaped courtyard and fountain assembly, this central wing houses the university's core directorates, executive offices, and administrative services.
