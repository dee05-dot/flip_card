NSUT APJ Lecture Complex

APJ Abdul Kalam Block

The Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Lecture Theatre Complex stands as the state-of-the-art academic heart of the campus. Featuring multi-tiered, amphitheater-style smart classrooms and massive high-capacity presentation halls, it hosts the university's premier orientation programs and grand induction events. Its premium audio-visual infrastructure and world-class design deliver a true university-level experience for incoming engineering students.
